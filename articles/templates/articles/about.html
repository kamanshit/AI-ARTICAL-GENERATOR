About AI Article Generator

AI Article Generator is a Django application that uses Generative AI to create articles from user-provided topics.

The application combines web research, embeddings, vector search, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to produce research-based articles.

Users can also chat with their generated articles, ask questions, and save their favorite articles to their personal collection.
